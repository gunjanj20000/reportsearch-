DISCHARGE SUMMARY & CATH REPORT — CASE #3006

Patient Diagnosis: Complex CAD with Management of Drug-Eluting In-Stent Restenosis
Primary Operator: Dr. Interventionalist, MD
Procedure Performed: Percutaneous Coronary Intervention with ISR

PROCEDURE DESCRIPTION:
Selective angiogram confirmed severe narrowing. Pathophysiological mechanisms of In-Stent Restenosis (ISR) including neoatherosclerosis and stent under-expansion. Treatment with drug-coated balloons (DCB) versus repeat stenting with bioresorbable scaffolds.
Lesion preparation was initiated with semi-compliant balloons.
Intravascular ultrasound (IVUS) confirmed reference diameter of 3.5mm.
Drug-eluting stent deployed with post-dilation at 18 atm.
Final angiogram showed TIMI 3 flow without dissection or distal embolization.
