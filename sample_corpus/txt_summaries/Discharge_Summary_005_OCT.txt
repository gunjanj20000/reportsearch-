DISCHARGE SUMMARY & CATH REPORT — CASE #3004
============================================================
Patient Diagnosis: Complex CAD with Optical Coherence Tomography High-Resolution Plaque Assessment
Primary Operator: Dr. Interventionalist, MD
Procedure Performed: Percutaneous Coronary Intervention with OCT

PROCEDURE DESCRIPTION:
Selective angiogram confirmed severe narrowing. Optical Coherence Tomography (OCT) with 10-micron axial resolution. Differentiation of thin-cap fibroatheroma (TCFA), plaque erosion, macrophage infiltration, and stent strut malapposition.
Lesion preparation was initiated with semi-compliant balloons.
Intravascular ultrasound (IVUS) confirmed reference diameter of 3.5mm.
Drug-eluting stent deployed with post-dilation at 18 atm.
Final angiogram showed TIMI 3 flow without dissection or distal embolization.
