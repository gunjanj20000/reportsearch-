Patient Diagnosis: Complex CAD with Chronic Total Occlusion PCI Techniques and Outcomes
Primary Operator: Dr. Interventionalist, MD
Procedure Performed: Percutaneous Coronary Intervention with CTO

PROCEDURE DESCRIPTION:

Final angiogram showed TIMI 3 flow without dissection or distal embolization.
